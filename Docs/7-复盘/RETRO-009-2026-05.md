## Problems

### Issue BK: three multi-symptom mount-staleness incidents

v3.6.1 had five dirty files and a damaged index. v3.6.2 truncated Profile.jsx. v3.6.3 had 18 stale files, contaminated permissions, and missing-file listings that refreshed automatically. The recorded root cause was Cowork mount POSIX behavior combined with cross-tool stale views. At that time, the reset-and-double-verify defense was reported to prevent business incidents in all three trials. The safety override above supersedes its historical preapproval language.

### Issue BP: a 401 was incorrectly escalated toward the provider

During F-WEEKLY-1 smoke testing, PM initially attributed a live-LLM 401 to a structural Xiaomi MiMo issue such as quota, revocation, or compliance. zlbdh explained that the key had been reset four times and the current value forgotten. BP was downgraded. PM correction 49 and issue BR showed that upstream identity had not been checked before speculation.

### Issue BR: model-provider identity was assumed incorrectly

PM had assumed xiaomimimo.com with an /anthropic interface was a third-party relay. zlbdh supplied a screenshot identifying Xiaomi's official, internally developed MiMo service and its domestic compliance context. The facts were recorded in `agent/shared/品牌词典.md` and cross-chat memory, with a candidate extension to Q4. Preserve the real provider attribution rather than inventing a different origin.

## Lessons

1. **Cowork mounts constrain concurrent cross-tool work.** Concurrent Git/file writes with Codex or Claude Code could contaminate permissions and cached views without altering the actual pushed commit. The historical response was double verification plus a preapproved reset path; current use must follow the safety override.
2. **Verify before inferring.** Code or interface shape does not establish provider identity or operational cause. Consult documentation, inspect evidence, or ask zlbdh first. BP and BR showed the same failure pattern.
3. **Evolve roles around workflow needs, not a corporate chart.** After zlbdh asked whether to imitate a real company, PM documented three expansion questions in `agent/shared/framework-演化哲学.md`: actual pain, frequency, and whether a sub-mode can suffice. This became a lasting decision principle.
