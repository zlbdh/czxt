## 3. Issue CC promoted to ADR-030 ✅

The main retained lesson: four device-tested token-budget cases—F-DEVIATION-2, F-NIGHT-1, F-F1, and F-F2+F-F4—led to **permanent ADR-030**, the 13th meta-rule.

- Evidence-driven acceptance: passing unit tests does not mean passing on the device.
- Token budgets: do not rely on memory; choose a sufficiently large value directly and let device results decide.
- A thinking-model.test is required.
