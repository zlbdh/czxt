## 4. Key findings

### 1. Project PM's external identity is a real recurring weakness: ADR-031

The pattern recurred five times after #63: unauthorized attribution #74, deferring decisions #77, excessive text #80, ambiguous reports #83, and internal codes #86. Six occurrences reached the firm threshold. After formalizing five rules, the expectation was **zero recurrences in Sprint-11**, still awaiting verification.

### 2. Health-check blind spots weaken framework self-governance: ADR-032

All three appeared during governance work:

- #88: recommended deleting historical records, nearly violating ADR-007.
- #89: ignored section byte sizes and risked damaging frequently used core content by forcing a split.
- #90: README checking missed 00_总入口/AGENTS/INDEX.

The systematic health-check SOP combines three sets of records with three assessment dimensions to strengthen safeguards.

ADR-031 rule 2 was being internalized through practice.
