# PM Self-Correction #59 — Operating System PM Crossed into Project PM's Workspace

## Summary

Citing framework consistency and the PROP-029 v2 physical split, Operating System PM proposed moving `项目PM/` into `操作系统/05_记忆/`. This exceeded its authority over another PM's private meta-memory directory.

## Trigger

**Time:** 2026-05-21 17:50, during a file-by-file project-root review.

1. Acting as Operating System PM, the PM noticed conceptual overlap between `项目PM/` and `操作系统/05_记忆/`.
2. Proposed merging the former into the latter for consistency with PROP-029 v2.
3. zlbdh asked why the Project PM's own directory was being equated with the operating system.
4. PM immediately recognized the authority violation.

## Causes

- Framework consistency was allowed to override PM role boundaries.
- `项目PM/` was Project PM's private meta-memory center, created under PROP-023, not an area Operating System PM could reorganize.
- Its organization belonged to Project PM; Operating System PM could not cross that boundary.

## Implemented safeguard

Added a private-meta-memory boundary section to `操作系统/01_架构/角色边界.md`, with four rules and two trigger scenarios:

1. No PM subrole may organize, move, rename, or merge another subrole's private meta-memory directory.
2. Framework consistency cannot override role boundaries.
3. Cross-PM private-directory coordination requires PROP governance plus the owning PM's decision.
4. Project PM may dispatch other PMs to organize their own directories, but must not move those directories directly.

## Repeated pattern

- #38/#41/#42, May 13–14, issue AJ origins: acting without switching roles.
- #55/#56, May 20: incorrect role identification.
- #59: the correct role was selected, but another role's territory was still crossed; a new dimension requiring a stronger prohibition.

## Meta-rule candidate

The private-directory prohibition is already implemented as a mandatory role-boundary rule. It does not need promotion into the meta-rule pool because it is part of the path rules.
