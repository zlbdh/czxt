# PM Self-Correction #60 — Naming Lagged Behind Design Intent

## Summary

`PM记忆中枢/` meant a PM memory center, but actually contained GTM work, pending decisions, and PRD drafts. Its name implied a much narrower scope than its contents.

## Trigger

**Time:** 2026-05-21 18:30.

1. PM finished issue CM v2.1: renaming `中枢/` to `PM记忆中枢/` for clarity and alignment with `操作系统/05_记忆/`.
2. zlbdh pointed out that this was a PM workspace containing more than memory, and Operations PM should also be included.
3. PM recognized that the earlier May 21 name considered only meta-memory, overlooking intended future GTM work, PRD drafts, and pending decisions.

## Causes

- Naming considered only then-known content: quick references, field reviews, and self-corrections.
- It ignored expansion into Operations PM GTM work, Product PM PRD drafts, and cross-PM decisions.
- Combining “center” and “memory” restricted the name's meaning rather than improving it.

## Safeguards

- Replace `PM记忆中枢/` with `PM工作区/` in v3. The broader workspace concept accommodates memory, work, and decisions.
- Add a table in `PM工作区/README.md` explicitly showing permitted contents beyond memory.

- Trigger: the current name does not match actual contents.
- Action: immediately reflect and propose an improved name; do not simply tolerate the mismatch.
- Evidence: #60.
