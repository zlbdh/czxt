# PM Self-Correction #61 — Role and Workspace Names Diverged from the Start

## Summary

When defining issue BI's role on May 19, PM named the role file `增长PM-推广咪咪.md` but the workspace `运营PM/`. The inconsistency existed from the start and lasted two days until zlbdh questioned it.

## Trigger

**Time:** 2026-05-21 19:00, issue CM v3.1.

1. In CM v3's workspace consolidation, including Operations PM, PM chose `增长PM-推广咪咪` for the merged directory, following the May 19 role-definition filename.
2. zlbdh asked why it was called Growth PM/Promotion Mimi instead of Operations PM/Operations Mimi.
3. On review, zlbdh's May 19 request had explicitly included operations, promotion, marketing, planning, and short video. PM invented “growth,” a term associated with B2B internet growth, for the role file while using the user's “operations” wording for the workspace.
4. Both names coexisted from May 19 through May 21 without PM noticing.

## Causes

- The role name replaced the user's wording with PM's own assumption.
- The workspace retained the user's operations wording, creating two names.
- During consolidation, PM reused the role filename without checking it against the user's wording and workspace name.
- No proactive naming-consistency check occurred; the discrepancy survived for two days until questioned.

## Safeguards

- Use the same name for a role-definition file and its workspace from the start.
- Treat zlbdh's wording as the reference; do not invent substitute terminology.
- Candidate decision-checkpoint Q4: naming-consistency verification.

## Meta-rule candidate

**Candidate #13: role-definition and workspace names must match from the start.**

- Trigger: creating a PM role or workspace.
- Action: use zlbdh's wording, match both names, and verify through decision-checkpoint Q4.

## Repeated pattern

- Similar to #54's conflict between handoff Step 8 and AC2: both lack internal-consistency checks.
