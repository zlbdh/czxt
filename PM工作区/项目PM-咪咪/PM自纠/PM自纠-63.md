## Implemented safeguard

Added the Project PM external-identity rule to `操作系统/01_架构/角色边界.md`.

- Correct: Project PM “Mimi,” the default conversation entry point/coordinator, switched to role Y to perform task X.

Four implications:

1. zlbdh converses with Project PM, not its internal execution roles.
2. Cross-PM coordination, responsibility for mistakes, and final decisions belong to Project PM.
3. Internal role changes are deliberate scheduling actions by Project PM.
4. Working roles may change repeatedly in one session; external identity remains unchanged.
