# PM Self-Correction #72 — Knowledge PM Belongs at the Meta Level ⭐⭐⭐

## Summary

zlbdh questioned why Knowledge PM was a nested implementation role when it served the entire project. Its level had been underestimated; it needed a meta-level position close to Project PM and spanning the full lifecycle.

## Trigger

**Time:** 2026-05-22 03:00, when zlbdh uploaded a locally edited v4.0 vision under uploads/.

1. The third revision, #68, placed Knowledge PM alongside execution and verification in the nested-subrole layer.
2. The user's question prompted review of its duties: coordinating all nine PMs through central Layer 2 knowledge, maintaining the project-wide Layer 3 view, and governing the meta-rule pool.
3. Those were project-wide meta-level duties, not implementation duties.

## Causes

- PM equated retaining knowledge with implementing a retention task and assigned it to implementation.
- Knowledge retention spans cross-PM coordination, meta-rule governance, and full-lifecycle reflection.
- The historical comparison cited BMAD Scrum Master and Letta self-managing memory hygiene as meta-agent roles rather than implementers.

## Implemented safeguards

Rewrote the final design in `操作系统/06_工具治理/操作系统终态愿景-v4.0-2026-05-21.md`:

1. Changed three levels, lead/subrole/nested-subrole, into four: lead/meta/subrole/nested-subrole.
2. Assigned Knowledge PM an independent meta-level position near Project PM, at or above the five decision PMs.
3. Defined three core duties: central knowledge, project knowledge, and meta-rule-pool governance.
4. Left only Development PM and Test and Release PM in the nested implementation layer.

## Repeated pattern

- Evolution: #65 established the dedicated role, #68 defined four categories, and #72 elevated it to the meta level.
- Linked to #71: project knowledge Layer 3 is an application of meta-level responsibility.

## Meta-rule candidate

**Issue DD: PMs with meta-level duties belong close to Project PM, outside the implementation layer.**

- Trigger: a role spans cross-PM coordination, meta-rule governance, and the entire project lifecycle.
- Action: establish it at the meta level, near Project PM and independent of the subrole/nested-subrole hierarchy.
