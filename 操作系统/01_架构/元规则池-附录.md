# Meta-Rule Pool Appendix

> Frequent-use entry: [meta-rule pool](元规则池.md). This appendix holds explanations and evolution history.

## 1. Relationship to other records

Ordinary rules from daily PROP records, Sprint decisions, and handoff cards can be changed or retired. After at least three occurrences of the same pattern and evaluation, they become candidate meta-rules in the issue-letter index A–DE+. Sprint RETRO evaluation and a PROP draft lead to an ADR, a permanent record. Recording the result in the meta-rule pool makes it mandatory across Sprints, PMs, and tools.

## 2. Promotion path

1. PM self-correction records one practical lesson.
2. The same pattern occurs at least three times across Sprints.
3. Register a candidate meta-rule in the issue-letter index.
4. Evaluate it at Sprint RETRO: the Knowledge PM leads the draft and the Project PM decides.
5. Draft a PROP and obtain zlbdh approval.
6. Create the permanent ADR in `Docs/3-开发文档/adr/`.
7. Record it in the meta-rule pool as permanent.
8. Enforce it across Sprints, PMs, and tools.
