# Meta-Rule Pool · Permanent Project Collaboration Rules

> **Highest-level collaboration rules**: mandatory across Sprints, PMs, and tools.  
> **Governance PM**: 🪞 Knowledge PM “Curator”; Project PM “Mimi” makes the final decision. See [candidates](元规则池-候选.md) and [history](元规则池-附录.md).

---

## 1. Definition

The **meta-rule pool** is the single source of truth for permanent project collaboration rules. Ordinary rules become permanent meta-rules only after verification through PM self-correction, RETRO, PROP, and ADR.

---

| **G** | Follow the PRD: application work starts from a PRD; PMs do not invent tasks. | Any feature implementation | ADR-022 decision 5 |

## 4. Promotion conditions

1. The same pattern recurs at least three times across Sprints.
2. Practical use confirms that the rule works.
3. Applicability across PMs and tools is clear.
4. Sprint RETRO approves it: the Knowledge PM drafts the proposal and the Project PM decides.

## 5. Governance process
