# Handoff Format Appendix

This appendix holds occasional explanations. See the [main format](交接卡格式.md) for the frequent-use template.

## Why the short chat handoff is required

- A PM or owner reading chat may not immediately open the full card.
- The short format lets the receiver quickly decide whether to confirm or follow up.
- Over time, sections ①-⑦ provide a low-cost view of cross-PM workflow status.

## Do not replace it with a summary paragraph

Even a detailed prose summary of files, tests, and decisions cannot replace ①-⑦:

- A summary has no fixed fields, so a PM cannot spot the key information immediately.
- Numbering ①-⑦ aligns with the full file's required ①-⑥ and the PM-transition record.
- Failure case: F-PROFILE-12 received a long summary that left the PM unsure what to confirm or follow up. This triggered PROP-014.

## Mandatory section ⑦: PM transitions

Historical trigger: repeated issue AJ recording failures showed that PM self-observation fades as a soft rule.

Requirements:

1. Every implementation-completion or cross-role short chat handoff includes section ⑦.
2. State “Added N rows this session (`状态.md L<line>`)”.
3. If N=0 but the session changed framework files, add the missing PM-transition record in `状态.md` before reporting.
4. Simple conversation does not need a short handoff. If a hook incorrectly classifies it as completion, use `N=0 / no role switch this session` to explain that no role transition occurred.

The template turns a soft reminder into a required trigger, preventing gaps in PM records.

## Mandatory-use matrix

| Action | Full card | Short chat handoff ①-⑦ |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-role transition or implementation completion | Required | Required |
| Internal PM progress | `状态.md` is sufficient | Optional |
| Temporary discussion | Not needed | Not needed |

After zlbdh pastes the short handoff into another session, the receiver should be able to resume within one minute by reading the full card in `交接区/待接手/`.

## Why automation does not silently rewrite semantic content

Automation is deliberately narrow:

- `chat-summary-check` checks for ①-⑦, the section ⑥ path, and the section ⑦ line number. It blocks or warns on failure; it does not rewrite the reply.
- `handoff-zone-check` checks pending cards' ordered `## ①` through `## ⑥` headings, five-state section ⑤ `Status`, backlog, and broken status links. It does not automatically move cards from `待接手/` to `已接手/`.
- `pm-tracking-check` checks PM-record freshness in `状态.md`; it does not append PM-transition rows on a PM's behalf.
- `readme-index-check` checks list/count consistency. A corresponding Apply is permitted only for deterministic indexes.
- The ADR watcher automatically maintains only deterministic tables such as the ADR README.

Card moves, status summaries, PM records, and the final seven-part chat handoff are semantic evidence. The Project PM's main session must determine what actually finished, who takes over, and who owns the next step. Hooks must not guess.

## Backward compatibility

Older three-section cards from before PROP-009 — completed work, next steps, and cautions — remain readable. New cards require at least ①-⑥, and pending cards must use ordered headings `## ①` through `## ⑥`. Status inference item 5 recommends upgrading old cards to the six required sections during the next implementation task.

Early accepted cards from 2026-06-14/15 may retain `status: HANDOFF`. Do not bulk-rewrite historical event records; use the historical boundary and age-based archival. Newly written or newly accepted cards follow `pending` → `accepted`.
