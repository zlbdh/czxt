## Maintenance rules

- When an issue permanently closes or becomes an ADR, update the ADR/RETRO/PROP source first, then consider whether the snapshot needs a refresh.
- Do not rewrite an old snapshot's collection date to chase current numbers. Explain drift with a pointer in this entry; do not backfill historical body text.
