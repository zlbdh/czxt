# Cross-branch Collaboration

Applies to **Project PM Mimi**, the inward product/development branch in the nine-PM matrix; **Operations Mimi**, the outward business/growth branch; and future PM branches.

“Branch” means a PM responsibility branch, not a Git branch. The code repository retains a single `main` strategy.

Triggers include cross-branch information flow, conflicting decisions, shared resources, persona consistency, and feedback into the product. Related: [handoff format](../../操作系统/03_交接/交接卡格式.md) and [ADR-023 on PM role subclasses](../../Docs/3-开发文档/adr/ADR-023-议题AJ落地-PM角色子类化+decision-checkpoint.md).

## 1. Responsibility structure

zlbdh is the decision-maker and sole arbitrator.

- The inward branch, Project PM Mimi, coordinates Operating System, Product, Technical, and Test PMs; Knowledge PM; and Development / Test and Release implementation roles.
- The outward branch, Operations Mimi, covers GTM. Future roles may include Customer Support, Commercialization, or Community PMs.

Operations PM “Operations Mimi” is an outward GTM exception and is not also assigned to the inward branch.

## 3. Cross-branch handoff area

Under `交接区/分支间/`, alongside the existing pending/accepted handoff areas:

- `项目PM→运营咪咪/待处理/` and `项目PM→运营咪咪/已处理/`: pending and processed Project-to-Operations cards.
- `运营咪咪→项目PM/待处理/` and `运营咪咪→项目PM/已处理/`: pending and processed Operations-to-Project cards.

The design follows the PROP-011 event stream.

## 4. Common scenarios

| Scenario | Initiator | Route | Decision authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| A ship, ADR, or RETRO can become content | Project PM | Project-to-Operations pending card | Operations chooses platform and depth. |
| User feedback suggests a product improvement | Operations | Operations-to-Project pending card | Project PM routes to Product PM for Sprint planning. |
| Content includes app screenshots, code, or private information | Operations | Content review card | **zlbdh approval**, with a confidentiality veto. |
| Persona inconsistency | Either | Suggest a change; Operating System PM writes shared assets. | zlbdh approval. |
| Decision or priority conflict | Either | Each reports to zlbdh in its own chat. | **zlbdh arbitrates.** |

## 7. Arbitration principles

- Confidentiality versus exposure: Operations yields; zlbdh has the confidentiality veto.
- Product cadence versus content timing: Project PM determines shipping time; Operations determines content publication time without mutual interference.
- Persona disagreements: the persona baseline is authoritative; the deviating party corrects itself.
- Resource and investment conflicts: zlbdh arbitrates.
- Path violations: the violating party stops immediately and corrects its playbook, following issue AJ's lesson.

## 8. Closure and expansion

After three to five weeks of phase B use, zero path violations, and at least five cross-branch handoffs without conflict, a RETRO evaluates whether to create an ADR. Choose its number from the ADR README; never reuse ADR-024.

Future Customer Support or Commercialization PMs, or weekly alignment, use the same event stream. Structural gaps require a new PROP.

## 9. Prohibited behavior

- Editing the other party's exclusive directories: Operations does not edit `{{APP_REPO_DIR}}/` or `Docs/3+7`; Project PM does not edit the Operations workspace.
- Passing cross-branch information only in chat without a handoff record.
- Bypassing the handoff area to place content directly in another PM's private workspace.
- Directly editing another PM's playbook; submit suggestions for zlbdh's approval.
- Creating or forking a separate project-memory source of truth.
- Arbitrating conflicts independently; zlbdh's authority cannot be substituted.
